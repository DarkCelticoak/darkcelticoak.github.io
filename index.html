Deal Finder

A private price-comparison tool for groups of community members in Ireland.

What it does

  • Keeps a list of products our members want to buy.
  • Compares their prices across Irish retailers.
  • Sends a message when a price drops, with a link to the cheapest store to buy from.

Product areas

Toys and games, electronics and electrical, smart home, audio, jewellery, footwear, clothing and craft supplies.

How we work with retailers

  • We prefer official product feeds and data services provided by retailers.
  • We only read store websites where the store allows it, and we follow each site's robots.txt.
  • Members always buy directly from the retailer.

Who can use it

Deal Finder is invite-only and is not open to the public. This page exists to describe the project to our affiliate partners, including Awin.
